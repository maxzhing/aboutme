Does it work from your network?

Connection check

Market-data APIs do not send CORS headers, so the app tries them directly first and then through public relays. AI providers are checked with a one-token ping. This runs from your browser, so it tells you what you can reach.

If every source is blocked

Paste history instead

Export daily bars from Yahoo, Stooq or your broker and paste them here — Date,Open,High,Low,Close,Volume, oldest or newest first. The whole analysis runs on pasted data exactly as it does on live data. Needs 60 rows, ideally 500+.

Export / import

Everything — portfolio, trade log, predictions, learned weights, API keys — lives in this browser's local storage. Nothing is sent anywhere except the market-data and AI providers you choose.
