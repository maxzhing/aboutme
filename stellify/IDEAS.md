# Extra ideas (proposals only; not built)

Ranked by impact per unit of effort.

1. **SAT-only "lite" download (about 1 MB).** One file without the AP courses, pdf.js and Recharts, for students on
   2G or with little storage. It matters because the audience is on cheap phones with spotty internet, and today's
   single file is 6.4 MB. Effort: S, mostly a build flag.
2. **"Why did you pick it?" one-tap reason on wrong answers.** After a miss, the student taps one of three short reasons
   that match the option's misconception tag, or "something else". It matters because it is the cheapest way to check
   that the N1 misconception tags match real student thinking, and the data stays on the device. Effort: S.
3. **Printable weekly review sheet.** One page of due reviews and confident errors, laid out like a booklet, with
   the answer key on the back. It matters because many students study away from a screen, or share one phone. Effort: S–M.
