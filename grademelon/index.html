Getting your grades in here

  1. Open StudentVUE and go to Grades.
  2. Pick the class and marking period you want.
  3. Select the assignment list and copy it — Ctrl + A then Ctrl + C works (⌘ on a Mac).
  4. Come back here and just press Ctrl + V. Anywhere on the page.
That's it — you don't have to click anything first. Grade Melon spots a gradebook the moment you paste it and skips the menus, headers and week rows that come along for the ride.

How the grade is worked out

MCPS uses weighted categories. Each category is scored on its own points, and then the two are combined by weight:

Practice / Preparation   =  PP earned ÷ PP possible     → weight 10%
All Tasks / Assessments  =  AT earned ÷ AT possible     → weight 90%

Marking period grade = (PP% × 10 + AT% × 90) ÷ 100

That is not the same as adding up every point. An 8-point Practice assignment barely moves your grade; an 8-point All Task moves it nine times as much.

Empty categories drop out. No Practice grades yet? All Tasks carries 100% of your grade on its own — the weights re-scale exactly the way Synergy does it.

Questions
Where does my data go?
Nowhere. It stays in this browser on this device. No account, no server, no upload. Clearing your browser data clears your classes — use Export at the bottom of the page first if you want a backup.
Do I need my StudentVUE password?
No — and don't type your school password into any site that isn't your school's. Grade Melon only reads what you paste.
It doesn't match StudentVUE exactly
Three usual causes. Ungraded work — Synergy leaves it out until a score exists, and so does this; check a teacher hasn't put in a zero you didn't copy. Weights — a few courses don't use 90/10; you can change them in Class settings. Dropped or excused work — untick it here to match.
What's a what-if score?
Any score you change or add here is a what-if. Drag a slider, type a number, or add an assignment that doesn't exist yet — the grade updates instantly and the row is marked. Reset puts your real grades back.
What does the small number on the right mean?
How much that one assignment is moving your grade right now. −2.10 means dropping it would raise you 2.1 points; +1.44 means it is pulling you up.
Keyboard shortcuts
Ctrl/⌘ + V — paste a gradebook from anywhere on the page. Ctrl/⌘ + Z — undo the last delete.
