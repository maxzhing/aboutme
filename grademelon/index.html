Semester grade calculator

MCPS builds a semester grade from your two marking period letter grades — not from averaging percentages. Each letter is worth quality points (A = 4, B = 3, C = 2, D = 1, E = 0); the two are averaged and a tie (a .5) rounds up.

How to get your grades in here

  1. Open StudentVUE / Synergy and go to Grades (Gradebook).
  2. Pick the class and marking period you want, so the assignment list is on screen.
  3. Select the whole assignment list — click just above the first assignment and drag to the bottom, or press Ctrl + A (⌘ + A on Mac) — then Ctrl + C to copy.
  4. Come back here, hit Paste gradebook on the class, and press Ctrl + V in the box.
  5. Hit Import. Every assignment lands in the right category with its points, and your grade appears.
Extra text — menus, headers, week rows, the class name — is fine. Grade Melon skips anything that is not an assignment.

How the grade is calculated

MCPS uses weighted categories. Each category is scored on its own — total points you earned over total points possible in that category — and then the categories are combined by weight:

Practice / Preparation   =  PP points earned ÷ PP points possible     → weight 10%
All Tasks / Assessments  =  AT points earned ÷ AT points possible     → weight 90%

Marking period grade = (PP% × 10 + AT% × 90) ÷ 100

This is not the same as adding up every point in the class. One 8-point Practice assignment does far less to your grade than one 8-point All Task.

Empty categories drop out. If you have no Practice/Preparation grades yet, All Tasks carries 100% of your grade on its own — the remaining weights are re-scaled, exactly the way Synergy does it. The same happens to any category you add.

Questions
Where is my data stored?
In your own browser, on this device only (localStorage). Nothing is uploaded, there is no account, and no server ever sees your grades. Clearing your browser data clears your classes — use Export first if you want a backup.
Do I need my StudentVUE login?
No, and you should never type your school password into a site that is not your school's. Grade Melon works entirely off what you copy and paste.
Why doesn't this match StudentVUE exactly?
Check three things. (1) Ungraded assignments — Synergy leaves them out until a score exists, and so does Grade Melon; make sure a teacher hasn't entered a zero you didn't import. (2) Category weights — a few courses use weights other than 90/10; you can edit them per class. (3) Excused or dropped assignments — untick them here to match.
What is a "what-if" assignment?
A fake assignment you add to see what would happen. It shows up pink with a WHAT-IF tag and counts in your grade so you can watch the number move. Clear what-ifs removes them all at once. Editing a real assignment's score works the same way — it gets an orange EDITED tag and a ↺ button to put the real score back.
What does the Impact column mean?
How much that single assignment is moving your grade right now — the difference between your grade with it and your grade without it. A −2.1 means dropping that assignment would raise you 2.1 points.
