This is not a summary of the topic. It is a triage document for one learner:

- `priorities` must be ordered by what will move this learner's outcome most —
  weak concepts and logged misconceptions first, solid material last or omitted.
- Say plainly where they currently stand on each priority.
- The condensed explanations should re-teach the weak items, not re-list them.
- `self_test` must be retrieval practice on the priorities, not recognition.
- If they have limited time, say what to skip.
