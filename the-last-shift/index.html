      Night shift · Site Kestrel, Sublevel 6 · 02:00 AM
      Survive until 6:00 AM

      You are the night guard, buried under the mountain. Something tall and pale has broken out of its cell, the facility is in lockdown, and the surface lift stays sealed until the day shift arrives at 6:00 AM.

      Your mission: stay alive until dawn — then make it onto the lift. Every hour it gets worse. It crawls. It hears everything. At 4:00 the power goes, and in the last hour it can follow you into the vents. Lockers only buy you time.

      Want out sooner? Find the three keycards, restore main power and upload the breach report, and you can open the lift yourself.

      Surviving the Warden

      • The mission: survive until 6:00 AM (the clock is at the top of the screen), then get onto the surface lift when the day shift opens it. Every hour it gets faster and sharper-eared; at 4:00 a power surge blacks everything out; in the last hour it can crawl into the vents. Or open the lift early: three keycards, main power and the breach report.
      • It hunts by sound and sight — and it has sharp ears. Walking near it is enough; when it hears you it runs to the sound. Crouch close to it, and hide the moment it starts running.
      • Your flashlight makes you visible from far away — and shining it at the Warden enrages it.
      • Its eyes give it away: pale when wandering, amber when investigating, red when hunting.
      • Break line of sight, close doors behind you and hide in lockers. It only finds you in a locker if it watched you get in — but you can only hold your nerve so long in there, and late at night it starts tearing lockers open.
      • Crouch to crawl through the maintenance vents. Until 5:00 they are too small for it.
      • Cornered? Fight. Punches won't kill it, but they stagger it, and a stun buys you a few seconds. It shrugs off a second stun for a while — and comes back angrier.
      • The heartbeat, flickering lights and the threat eye (top right) tell you when it is close.
      • Sometimes it just watches you from the dark. Hold its gaze and it backs off — walk toward it, or put your light on it, and it charges.
      • Read every note. They contain the codes, routes and secrets you need to get out.
      • Bonus goals (under your objectives): six voice logs hidden around the level tell its story; rebooting the cameras in Camera Control puts it on your minimap (the reboot is loud); and you can learn who it used to be. Every shift ends with a score and a rank, and 15 achievements are saved under Goals.
      • Co-op (up to 4): one player hosts and sends the invite link (or the 4-letter room code); friends open it and press Join. Keycards are shared, the Warden hunts whoever it notices, and a caught guard wakes up at Desk B — unless the whole team goes down.
