    Find the three department keycards, restore main power, download the containment breach report and reach the surface lift. Stay quiet. Stay out of its light.

    Controls

    W A S DMove (arrow keys also work) MouseLook — click START to capture the mouse ShiftSprint — fast, drains stamina, very loud Ctrl / CCrouch — hold Ctrl or toggle with C. Quiet, needed for vents.     Surviving the Warden

    • It hunts by sound and sight. Walking is audible, sprinting carries across the level, crouching is nearly silent.
    • Your flashlight makes you visible from far away — and shining it at the Warden enrages it.
    • Its eyes give it away: pale when wandering, amber when investigating, red when hunting.
    • Break line of sight, close doors behind you and hide in lockers. It only finds you in a locker if it watched you get in.
    • Maintenance vents are too small for it — crouch to crawl through.
    • The heartbeat, flickering lights and the threat eye (top right) tell you when it is close.
    • Read every note. They contain the codes, routes and secrets you need to get out.
