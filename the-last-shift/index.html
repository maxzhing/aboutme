      Night shift · Site Kestrel, Sublevel 6 · 02:00 AM
      Survive until 6:00 AM

      You are the night guard, buried under the mountain. Something tall and pale has broken out of its cell, the facility is in lockdown, and the surface lift stays sealed until the day shift arrives at 6:00 AM.

      Your mission: stay alive until dawn — then make it onto the lift. Every hour it gets worse. It crawls. It hears everything. At 4:00 the power goes, and in the last hour it can follow you into the vents. Lockers only buy you time.

      It is not alone. A little after 2:30 something else gets up out of the Morgue — slow, humming a lullaby. It never chases you, but stay away from it: near it you start to hallucinate, and you can't trust anything you see or hear.

      Want out sooner? Find the three keycards, restore main power and upload the breach report, and you can open the lift yourself.

      Someone has been taking pictures down here. Eight Polaroids are pinned to walls around Sublevel 6 — somewhere different every time you play. You start with a flashlight, and the Warden is already awake.

      Find all eight, then get to the surface lift. Every photo you take makes it worse: faster, sharper-eared, and at four the power fails. The Sleepwalker comes out after the second. The clock is running — beat your best time.

      The day shift is never coming. There is no lift, no keycard, no way out — only the night, and how long you can last in it.

      Every hour (about a minute and a half) you get a spare battery — and you must choose one of two curses. They stack: a faster Warden, sharper ears, brownouts, open vents, phantoms everywhere… Every run is different. Beat your longest night.

      Surviving the Warden

      • Three modes (pick one when you press New Game): The Night Shift — survive until 6:00 AM; The Polaroids — find 8 photos hidden somewhere new every run, each one making it worse, then race to the lift (there is a Daily layout to compete on); Endless Night — no dawn, a new curse every hour (press 1 or 2 to choose), survive as long as you can.
      • The Night Shift: survive until 6:00 AM (the clock is at the top of the screen), then get onto the surface lift when the day shift opens it. Every hour it gets faster and sharper-eared; at 4:00 a power surge blacks everything out; in the last hour it can crawl into the vents. Or open the lift early: three keycards, main power and the breach report.
      • It hunts by sound and sight — and it has sharp ears. Walking near it is enough; when it hears you it runs to the sound. Crouch close to it, and hide the moment it starts running.
      • Your flashlight makes you visible from far away — and shining it at the Warden enrages it.
      • Its eyes give it away: pale when wandering, amber when investigating, red when hunting.
      • Break line of sight, close doors behind you and hide in lockers. It comes for your locker if it watched you get in, if it lost you right next to it, or if it stops outside and decides to try the door — then you have to hold the door shut. And you can only hold your nerve so long in there.
      • Crouch to crawl through the maintenance vents. Until 5:00 they are too small for it.
      • Cornered? Fight. Punches won't kill it, but they stagger it, and a stun buys you a few seconds. It shrugs off a second stun for a while — and comes back angrier.
      • Weapons. There's a crowbar on the security desk where you wake up. The other four glow orange where they lie and are marked orange on your map: a fire axe behind glass (Generator Hall), a shotgun (Containment — deafening, everything hears it), a flare gun (Camera Control) that sets it on fire and lights up the dark, and molotovs (Break Room) that leave a pool of fire it has to walk through. The bonus list under your objectives names the closest one you can reach, and H mentions it too. Face it with a real weapon in your hand (not the crowbar) and the Last Dance begins: it has a health bar now. Hits while it's staggered do extra damage; at half health it gets enraged. In the Night Shift, kill it and the shift is over. In the other modes nothing stays dead — it gets back up.
      • Hold the door. When it grabs your locker door, a strip of notes slides in: press Space every time a pull reaches the bar — on the beat. Mashing doesn't help. Miss too many and it's in. While it's at your door your nerve holds.
      • The heartbeat, flickering lights and the threat eye (top right) tell you when it is close.
      • Look up. In the long hallways it often climbs the wall and crawls along the ceiling, and it drops down on whoever is underneath. And it is cunning: a crash or a screech from one direction can be bait while it circles round to come at you from another.
      • Sometimes it just watches you from the dark. Hold its gaze and it backs off — walk toward it, or put your light on it, and it charges.
      • The Sleepwalker comes out of the Morgue at about 2:30. It is slow and it never hunts you — it just drifts from room to room humming a lullaby, and the lights dim around it. But near it you hallucinate: the room warps, you hear things that aren't there, the clock lies, and you see the Warden where it isn't (look straight at it and it vanishes). Never let it drift into you: it shrieks, your light dies — and the Warden hears.
      • On Hard the Warden breaks through locked iron security doors. Only the blast door to the lift holds.
      • Read every note. They contain the codes, routes and secrets you need to get out.
      • Bonus goals (under your objectives): six voice logs hidden around the level tell its story; rebooting the cameras in Camera Control puts it on your minimap (the reboot is loud); and you can learn who it used to be. Every shift ends with a score and a rank, and 24 achievements are saved under Goals, with your best times for every mode.
      • Co-op (up to 4): one player hosts and sends the invite link (or the 4-letter room code); friends open it and press Join. Keycards are shared, the Warden hunts whoever it notices, and a caught guard wakes up at Desk B — unless the whole team goes down.
