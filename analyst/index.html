What this desk actually computes

Short horizon — intraday to two weeks

Eleven detectors run on every bar and each casts one vote with a weight. Nothing is a black box: the ledger prints the reading that produced each vote and the sentence explaining it.

  • RSI(14), Wilder-smoothed — oversold below 30, overbought above 70, plus swing-based divergence against price.
  • MACD(12,26,9) — signal-line crossovers inside the last three bars; an expanding histogram adds weight.
  • Stochastic(14,3,3) slow — only counts when it agrees with RSI, which is what stops it firing on every chop.
  • ADX(14) with ±DI — a hard gate. Below 25 the trend-following votes are struck out entirely and only mean-reversion setups survive, capped at confidence 6.
  • OBV slope versus price slope over 10 bars — agreement confirms, disagreement is an early reversal warning.
  • VWAP — session-anchored on intraday bars, rolling 20-bar on daily bars. Which one ran is stated on the row.
  • Volume spike — any bar above 2× the 20-bar average. It never votes on its own; it multiplies the weight of whatever direction already leads.
  • MFI(14) — the volume-weighted read on the same momentum question.
  • Bollinger(20,2) bandwidth — the spec's absolute rule (<1%) and an adaptive one (bottom decile of 126 bars), reported separately so you can see which fired.
  • Swing structure — pivots clustered into support and resistance within 0.6 ATR, weighted by touches and recency.
  • Candlesticks at level — engulfing, pin bar and doji, counted only within half an ATR of a real level.

Long horizon — one month to two years+

The fundamental rubric is scored exactly as written in the spec, raw range −5 to +7, then mapped to ten points as round(10 × (raw + 5) / 12). That arithmetic is printed on the scorecard so the /10 is never mysterious. Fair value is a three-method blend — sector-multiple, PEG-anchored and a five-year discounted cash flow — and the methodology line names every method that had enough input to run, with its weight.

What it refuses to do

  • Fewer than three aligned signals → NO TRADE, regardless of how good the chart looks.
  • Reward-to-risk under 2:1 at T1 → NO TRADE. The target is never moved to manufacture the ratio.
  • A move over 5% in one session with no pullback → no chase.
  • Fundamental score under 5/10 → no long-term accumulation, and the recommended size prints 0%.
  • Drawdown of 10% from peak equity → all new entries halt until equity recovers.
  • Anything you did not supply is reported unknown and scored zero. It is never invented.
